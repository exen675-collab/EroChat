FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no card. Paid plans raise the limits.

Is Wink safe for work?

By default, yes. Mature content is off until an adult (18+) turns it on in settings, and it is never shown to anyone who hasn’t opted in.

Are my chats private?

Your conversations belong to your account. They are never shown publicly or used to train models.

Can I import characters from elsewhere?

Yes — Character Card V2 files (PNG or JSON) import out of the box.

Can I cancel anytime?

Once billing launches, yes. Cancel in one click and keep access until the end of the period.
